PROJECT 01 · FINAL YEAR PROJECT

Semi-Autonomous In-Pipe Inspection and Monitoring Robot

SNGPL-Sponsored Project | 2024–2025

Developed as a final-year engineering project to explore robotic inspection of gas pipelines. The project focused on a mobile inspection platform capable of navigating inside pipelines and collecting condition-monitoring information.

  • Worked on mechanical concept and chassis development.
  • Explored spring-loaded telescopic and pantographic leg mechanisms.
  • Designed for pipeline diameters of approximately 14–16 inches.
  • Included ultrasonic wall-thickness inspection and monitoring concepts.
  • Explored FPV, LoRa and GPS-based monitoring components.
